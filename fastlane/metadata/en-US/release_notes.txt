New in MacroMark 1.1

- Send watch text directly to a GitHub daily Markdown file, an HTTPS webhook, or both after setup on iPhone.
- Check pending deliveries and read replies from your configured GitHub repository on the watch. Open them from the Replies and deliveries complication.
- Watch audio transfers retry if the iPhone has not confirmed them after a day.

Phone and iCloud delivery remain the default. Audio recordings still use iPhone transcription. Direct Send keeps your text as captured, without expanding macros. MacroMark remains free.
